# Phase V.9 — The Keeper Measures the Monsters

Expansion monsters must be capable of crossing the future consumer bridge without being copied into a second monster model.

This refinement aligns the GMREXP monster definition with the established Companion Steward's Workshop / shared Bestiary vocabulary: identity and classification, AC, HP, movement, six ability scores, saves, skills, damage/condition defences, senses, languages, challenge, proficiency bonus, traits, actions, bonus actions, reactions, legendary actions, mythic features/actions, lair actions, player-safe description and Keeper notes.

The Review Desk exposes a friendly Bestiary contract for `monster` records. Blank fields remain absent: Pippin does not invent missing statistics. The existing canonical `MonsterSchemaFactory` and `MonsterStructureConstraint` remain authoritative.

GMREXP owns expansion monster definitions. Companion and Tabletop are future consumers of the same canonical expansion identity; this phase does not copy records into Companion, publish them, activate an expansion, or create encounter snapshots.

Per-monster WordPress media attachment IDs and Companion publication state are intentionally not part of the expansion contract. Expansion assets remain pack-owned; publication/activation remains at the expansion boundary.
